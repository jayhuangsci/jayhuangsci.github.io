I keep this site for things I'd like to remember the next time I open a project. A workout entry that says 205 lb leaves out which plates I put on the bar. A directory full of JSONL shards leaves open whether a Hadoop job finished writing them. Both details are easy to miss until you need the answer.

Most of my notes start there, with a small decision I can explain using a commit, a test, or an actual result. I also write about my coursework and research when there's something I want to work through in public.

The first two project series are [HabitTracker](/tags/habittracker/) and [Distdemo](/tags/distdemo/).
